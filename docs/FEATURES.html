The Nura App — Features & How to Use

A walkthrough of every major screen in the app, what it's for, and how to use it — with real screenshots captured from a running build. Screenshots with a real signed-in account had the name/email/avatar redacted for this doc; the screens themselves are otherwise unmodified.

This is user-facing feature documentation. For the technical architecture, see CLAUDE.md at the project root. For everything AI/ML-related, see the backend's AI_ML_OVERVIEW.md.

Getting started

Sign up

First name, last name, email, and a password (8+ characters) — or skip all of that with Continue with Google at the bottom, which is the fastest path in. Ticking "I agree to the Terms & Privacy Policy" is required before Create account activates.

Verify your email

Signing up with email (not Google) sends a 6-digit code that expires in 10 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, Resend unlocks after a short cooldown. After verifying, you're walked through a short onboarding (cycle length, last period date, goals) before landing in the app.

Log in

Returning users log in with email/password or Continue with Google. If you're joining as someone's partner rather than tracking your own cycle, there's a separate "partner connect" link here too (enter the invite code your partner shares from their Explore → Partner Sync screen).

Home — your daily dashboard

The first thing you see after logging in. Deliberately short — a snapshot, not a wall of tiles:

  • Greeting + notification bell at the top; a red dot means there's something unread (a partner message, a reminder, a like/comment on your post).
  • Streak and Care Points pills — the streak counts consecutive days you've logged something; Care Points are earned by logging, completing habits, and caring for your Forest tree (see below), spendable toward premium features/coupons.
  • Week strip — tap any day to jump straight into logging for that day.
  • Cycle-phase hero card — your current cycle day, phase (Period / Follicular / Ovulation / Luteal), and when your next period/fertile window is expected, all computed from your own logged history, not a fixed calendar guess.
  • Quick actions — Log period / Symptoms / Mood in one tap, each opening the day-log sheet pre-focused on that section.
  • Insights carousel — a couple of swipeable cards (cycle trend, length history); See all goes to the full Predictions screen.
  • "More for you" — three cards that group everything else into three hubs, so Home never grows back into a long list of tiles:
    • Care & Insights — predictions, daily insights, symptom trends/checker, reports, consults.
    • Wellness & Support — health concerns, Nura AI, hygiene, sexual wellbeing, check-in calls.
    • Explore — forest, blogs, videos, partner sync, habits, buddy nomination, govt. schemes.

Tracker — your cycle calendar

  • Cycle-day ring at the top shows exactly how many days until your next expected period, at a glance.
  • Full month calendar, color-coded by phase (the legend — Period / Follicular / Ovulation / Luteal — pins to the top once you scroll past it, so the colors stay readable no matter where you are). Small dots under a date mean something was logged that day.
  • Tap any date to open the day-log sheet: log flow, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, water, weight, temperature, stress, medication, digestion, skin, discharge, exercise, or a note — pick whichever sections apply, save, done. Nothing is required beyond what you actually want to record.
  • The search icon (top-right, magnifier) jumps straight to any feature in the app by name — cycle trend, next-period prediction, Ask Nura AI, and more — without hunting through hubs.
  • The gear icon opens Cycle Settings: reminder toggles (days-before, ovulation, fertility), your cycle length/period length, and tracking goal.

Community — anonymous peer support

A forum where everything you post can be shown anonymously (masked as "Anonymous member," never your real name) — toggle that per-post, or turn on Anonymous Community Mode app-wide from Profile → Preferences.

  • Category chips (All / PCOS / Fitness / Skin / …) filter the feed.
  • Posts can include polls — tap an option to vote, see live percentages.
  • The + button (bottom-right) starts a new post: text, an optional poll, optional anonymity, optional photo attachment.
  • Like, comment, or report any post from its row; report enough times and a post is automatically hidden pending review.

Profile — your account

  • Stat tiles — Days Tracked, Care Points, and your current Day Streak.
  • Achievements — badges for tracking milestones.
  • Preferences & Settings (expands in place) — Cycle Notifications toggle, Anonymous Community Mode toggle, and Gen Z Mode (a fun cosmetic toggle that swaps a handful of labels around the app into Gen Z slang — flip it back off any time, nothing else changes).
  • My Subscription — pricing, purchase history, and where premium features are unlocked (see "Premium features" below).
  • App Lock — PIN or biometric lock on top of your login, for extra privacy on a shared device.
  • Help & Support, Data Privacy & Security, and Log out.

The three hubs

Reached from Home's "More for you" cards. Each groups related features behind one entry point so Home stays short.

Care & Insights

  • Predictions — next period/ovulation forecasts with a confidence indicator (widens automatically if your cycles are irregular or you've gone a while without logging).
  • Daily Insights — a personalized hormone/mood/energy/skin note for today, plus suggestions (exercise, nutrition, sleep, general wellness).
  • Symptom Trends — which symptoms tend to show up in which cycle phase, based purely on your own logged history (unlocks after ~2 cycles of data).
  • Symptom Checker — a short questionnaire flagging possible PCOS, endometriosis, thyroid issues, or fibroids as "may be worth discussing with a clinician" — never a diagnosis.
  • Graphs & Reports — cycle length trend, weight/BBT, water intake and sleep averages, plus a downloadable doctor report.
  • Documents — your uploaded prescriptions/lab reports/bills, with AI summarize and chat-about-your-documents built in.

Wellness & Support

  • Nura AI — a private, judgment-free chat companion. Pick a topic (Periods & Cycle, Symptoms & Pain, Hygiene & Care, Mood & PMS, Sex & Intimacy — 18+ gated, Nutrition & Wellness, or Other) and it stays scoped to that topic; skip a topic for a general open chat. Past chats are saved and resumable from qa-history. Works by text or voice (tap the mic).
  • Health Concerns — browse/search common conditions and their info.
  • Hygiene & Care and Sexual Wellbeing — guidance content, same judgment-free framing.
  • Check-in Call — Buddy (the voice agent) calls your phone and walks you through a spoken check-in; can also be scheduled to recur automatically every cycle.

Explore

  • Forest — a personal-growth visual: a tree that grows (seed → sprout → sapling → tree → bloom) the more consistently you check in, and visibly droops if you skip a day. Purely a motivational mechanic layered on top of your regular logging.
  • Blogs and Videos — an articles/video library, filterable by category, translatable into 11 Indic languages + English.
  • Partner Sync — generate an invite code so a partner can connect, chat with them, and control exactly what cycle info they can see.
  • Habits — daily habit tracking with its own streak and reward points.
  • Nominate Buddy — anonymously pair with a peer for mutual support.
  • Govt. Schemes — a searchable directory of government reproductive/ menstrual-health benefit schemes, with an eligibility checker per scheme.

A closer look: Predictions & Graphs

Predictions shows your phase, a countdown ring to your next period, and — if your logged periods have started drifting from what's expected — a plain- language warning suggesting you re-anchor your settings, rather than quietly showing a forecast it no longer trusts. Graphs & Reports turns your last few months of logs into simple cards (cycle length bars, weight/BBT, water/ sleep averages) plus one-tap access to the downloadable report.

A closer look: Nura AI chat

Opens with a short intro and a few suggested starter questions. Ask anything in your own words — it answers from a curated knowledge base first, falls back to AI for anything not already covered, and always points you toward a real clinician if something sounds urgent. Nothing here is a diagnosis.

A closer look: Forest

Shows your care streak and trees-planted count, with your current tree front and center — the bounce when you log something, the droop when you don't, and a running history of past cycles' trees below.

Partner mode

If someone connects to your account as a partner (via Explore → Partner Sync's invite code), they get a different, read-only-by-default view when they log in: a shared overview of what you've chosen to share, a chat with you, the same voice assistant, the same community forum, and their own account settings — with its own floating tab bar so it's visually distinct from the owner's dashboard, but built from several of the same screens (Community in particular is byte-identical for both roles).

Premium features

A few features are gated behind a paywall, priced individually or as bundles — checked live against your account, never assumed free. When you hit a locked feature you'll see a preview + unlock prompt (PaywallModal) right there, with the price and a "purchase" flow (card/UPI/netbanking via Cashfree, with optional coupon codes). Profile → My Subscription is always available for browsing prices and past purchases outside of hitting a lock organically.

Notifications & reminders

The bell icon (Home and the partner-view overview) opens your notification list — partner messages, buddy chat, community likes/comments, and reminder nudges. Reminders themselves (period-coming-up, ovulation, and a daily "log today" nudge if you haven't yet) are configured from the Tracker's Cycle Settings sheet, and arrive as real push notifications once enabled.

Accessibility & personalization

  • Light/Dark mode — toggle from the top of most screens (sun/moon icon).
  • App Lock — PIN or biometric, layered on top of your session.
  • 11 languages — Nura AI, blogs, and voice mode all support English + 10 Indic languages.
  • Gen Z Mode (Profile → Preferences) — a purely cosmetic toggle that swaps some labels into Gen Z slang, for fun — flip it off any time and everything's back to normal instantly.
