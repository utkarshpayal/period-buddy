## Getting started

**Sign up** — first name, last name, email, and a password (8+ characters),
or skip straight in with **Continue with Google**. Creating an account
requires agreeing to the Terms & Privacy Policy.

**Verify your email** — signing up with email (not Google) sends a 6-digit
code that expires in 10 minutes; **Resend** unlocks after a short cooldown.
After verifying, a short onboarding walks you through your cycle length,
last period date, and tracking goal before you land in the app.

**Log in** — returning users log in with email/password or **Continue with
Google**. If you're joining as someone's partner rather than tracking your
own cycle, there's a separate "partner connect" link on the login screen
(enter the invite code your partner shares from their **Explore → Partner
Sync** screen).

## Home — your daily dashboard

The first screen after logging in. Deliberately short — a snapshot, not a
wall of tiles:

- **Greeting + notification bell** — a red dot means something's unread (a
  partner message, a reminder, a like/comment on your post).
- **Streak and Care Points** — the streak counts consecutive days you've
  logged something; Care Points are earned by logging, completing habits,
  and caring for your Forest tree, and are spendable toward premium
  features/coupons.
- **Week strip** — tap any day to jump straight into logging for that day.
- **Cycle-phase hero card** — your current cycle day, phase (Period /
  Follicular / Ovulation / Luteal), and when your next period/fertile window
  is expected, computed from your own logged history rather than a fixed
  calendar guess.
- **Quick actions** — Log period / Symptoms / Mood in one tap, each opening
  the day-log sheet pre-focused on that section.
- **Insights carousel** — a couple of swipeable cards (cycle trend, length
  history); **See all** goes to the full Predictions screen.
- **"More for you"** — three cards that group everything else into three
  hubs, so Home never grows back into a long list of tiles: Care & Insights,
  Wellness & Support, and Explore (each described below).

## Tracker — your cycle calendar

- **Cycle-day ring** at the top shows exactly how many days until your next
  expected period, at a glance.
- **Full month calendar**, color-coded by phase — the legend (Period /
  Follicular / Ovulation / Luteal) pins to the top once you scroll past it,
  so the colors stay readable no matter where you are. Small dots under a
  date mean something was logged that day.
- **Tap any date** to open the day-log sheet: log flow, symptoms, mood,
  energy, sleep, water, weight, temperature, stress, medication, digestion,
  skin, discharge, exercise, or a note — pick whichever sections apply, save,
  done. Nothing is required beyond what you actually want to record.
- **Search** (magnifier icon) jumps straight to any feature in the app by
  name — cycle trend, next-period prediction, Ask Nura AI, and more —
  without hunting through hubs.
- **Cycle Settings** (gear icon) — reminder toggles (days-before, ovulation,
  fertility), your cycle length/period length, and tracking goal.

## Community — anonymous peer support

A forum where everything you post can be shown anonymously (masked as
"Anonymous member," never your real name) — toggle that per-post, or turn on
**Anonymous Community Mode** app-wide from Profile → Preferences.

- **Category chips** (All / PCOS / Fitness / Skin / …) filter the feed.
- Posts can include **polls** — tap an option to vote and see live
  percentages.
- The **+ button** starts a new post: text, an optional poll, optional
  anonymity, optional photo attachment.
- Like, comment, or report any post from its row; enough reports auto-hide a
  post pending review.

## Profile — your account

- **Stat tiles** — Days Tracked, Care Points, and your current Day Streak.
- **Achievements** — badges for tracking milestones.
- **Preferences & Settings** — Cycle Notifications toggle, Anonymous
  Community Mode toggle, and **Gen Z Mode** (a fun cosmetic toggle that
  swaps a handful of labels around the app into Gen Z slang — flip it back
  off any time, nothing else changes).
- **My Subscription** — pricing, purchase history, and where premium
  features are unlocked.
- **App Lock** — PIN or biometric lock on top of your login, for extra
  privacy on a shared device.
- **Help & Support**, **Data Privacy & Security**, and **Log out**.

## Care & Insights hub

Reached from Home's "More for you" cards — everything data-driven about
your cycle:

- **Predictions** — next period/ovulation forecasts with a confidence
  indicator that widens automatically if your cycles are irregular or
  you've gone a while without logging.
- **Daily Insights** — a personalized hormone/mood/energy/skin note for
  today, plus suggestions (exercise, nutrition, sleep, general wellness).
- **Symptom Trends** — which symptoms tend to show up in which cycle phase,
  based purely on your own logged history (unlocks after ~2 cycles of
  data).
- **Symptom Checker** — a short questionnaire flagging possible PCOS,
  endometriosis, thyroid issues, or fibroids as "may be worth discussing
  with a clinician" — never a diagnosis.
- **Graphs & Reports** — cycle length trend, weight/BBT, water intake and
  sleep averages, plus a downloadable doctor report.
- **Documents** — your uploaded prescriptions/lab reports/bills, with AI
  summarize and chat-about-your-documents built in.

## Wellness & Support hub

- **Nura AI** — the private chat companion (see below for the full
  breakdown).
- **Health Concerns** — browse/search common conditions and their info.
- **Hygiene & Care** and **Sexual Wellbeing** — guidance content, with the
  same judgment-free framing.
- **Check-in Call** — Buddy (the voice agent) calls your phone and walks
  you through a spoken check-in; can also be scheduled to recur
  automatically every cycle.

## Explore hub

- **Forest** — a personal-growth visual: a tree that grows (seed → sprout →
  sapling → tree → bloom) the more consistently you check in, and visibly
  droops if you skip a day — a motivational mechanic layered on top of your
  regular logging.
- **Blogs** and **Videos** — an articles/video library, filterable by
  category, translatable into 11 Indic languages + English.
- **Partner Sync** — generate an invite code so a partner can connect, chat
  with them, and control exactly what cycle info they can see.
- **Habits** — daily habit tracking with its own streak and reward points.
- **Nominate Buddy** — anonymously pair with a peer for mutual support.
- **Govt. Schemes** — a searchable directory of government reproductive/
  menstrual-health benefit schemes, with an eligibility checker per scheme.

## Nura AI — the chat companion

A private, judgment-free chat you can reach from the Wellness & Support hub
or the tab bar's center avatar. Pick a topic (Periods & Cycle, Symptoms &
Pain, Hygiene & Care, Mood & PMS, Sex & Intimacy — 18+ gated, Nutrition &
Wellness, or Other) and it stays scoped to that topic for the whole
conversation; skip a topic for a general open chat. Past chats are saved
and resumable. Works by text or voice — tap the mic to speak instead of
typing, and it can read replies back to you in voice mode.

It answers from a curated knowledge base first, falls back to AI for
anything not already covered, and always points you toward a real
clinician if something sounds urgent. Nothing it says is a diagnosis.

## Partner mode

If someone connects to your account as a partner (via **Explore → Partner
Sync**'s invite code), they get a different, read-only-by-default view when
they log in: a shared overview of what you've chosen to share, a chat with
you, the same voice assistant, the same community forum, and their own
account settings — with its own floating tab bar so it's visually distinct
from the owner's dashboard, though several screens (Community in
particular) are identical for both roles.

## Premium features & subscriptions

A few features are gated behind a paywall, priced individually or as
bundles — checked live against your account, never assumed free. When you
hit a locked feature you'll see a preview and an unlock prompt right there,
with the price and a purchase flow (card/UPI/netbanking, with optional
coupon codes). **Profile → My Subscription** is always available for
browsing prices and past purchases outside of hitting a lock organically.

---

## Notifications & reminders

The bell icon opens your notification list — partner messages, buddy chat,
community likes/comments, and reminder nudges. Reminders themselves
(period-coming-up, ovulation, and a daily "log today" nudge if you haven't
yet) are configured from the Tracker's Cycle Settings sheet, and arrive as
real push notifications once enabled.

## Accessibility & personalization

- **Light/Dark mode** — toggle from the top of most screens.
- **App Lock** — PIN or biometric, layered on top of your session.
- **11 languages** — Nura AI, blogs, and voice mode all support English +
  10 Indic languages.
- **Gen Z Mode** (Profile → Preferences) — a purely cosmetic toggle that
  swaps some labels into Gen Z slang for fun — flip it off any time and
  everything's back to normal instantly.
