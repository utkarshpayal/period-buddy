# Nura — Features & Pitch

*A period & women's health companion app — plain-English feature list and the pitch for why it stands out.*

---

## The 30-second pitch

Most period trackers do one thing: they log your period and guess your next one. **Nura does that too — but it's built as a full women's health companion**, not just a calendar with a red dot.

In one app: an **AI assistant** that answers your health questions in plain language, **real doctors** you can video-call, a **partner mode** that turns your private logs into actual caring guidance for the people around you, a **medical document vault** that can read your lab reports and turn your cycle history into a doctor-ready PDF, a **gamified wellness tree** that makes healthy habits feel rewarding instead of like a chore, a **community** of people going through the same things, and — uniquely — a guide to **real government health schemes and benefits** you may be entitled to.

It's the difference between an app that tracks your body and an app that actually takes care of you.

## Everything it does, in plain English

### 🩸 Track your cycle, properly
- **Calendar & predictions** — see your period, fertile window, and ovulation predicted ahead of time, color-coded by phase.
- **Log your day in seconds** — one tap opens a colorful, icon-based log sheet covering everything: flow, mood (15 different moods, not just "happy/sad"), physical symptoms, ovulation tests, sex & sex drive, vaginal discharge, digestion, pregnancy tests, exercise, sleep, water, weight, temperature, stress, and medication.
- **Pregnancy Mode** — trying to conceive or already pregnant? Predictions pause and the app adjusts to support that instead.
- **Symptom Predictions** — "you're likely to get headaches around day 3 of your period" — based on *your own* history, not a generic chart.
- **Daily Insights** — a short, personal explanation each day of what's happening hormonally, plus tailored food/sleep/exercise tips for that exact phase.
- **Graphs & Reports** — a clean visual dashboard of your cycle length, weight, temperature, water intake, and sleep trends over time.
- **Symptom Checker** — a guided questionnaire that flags possible signs of PCOS, endometriosis, fibroids, or thyroid issues early, and keeps a history of past check-ins.

### 🤖 AI that actually helps
- **Nura AI Assistant** — a chat (and voice!) companion you can ask literally anything about periods, discharge, cramps, hygiene, intimacy, or body changes — and it tells you honestly when something needs a real doctor instead of just an app.
- **Chat with your own medical documents** — upload a lab report or prescription and *ask it questions*, in your own language, with the answer read back to you out loud if you want.
- **AI-written health report** — turns months of your logs and documents into a written summary a doctor can actually use.

### 📁 Your health records, organized
- **Document vault** — store prescriptions, lab reports, bills, and scans in one secure place, searchable and filterable.
- **Downloadable doctor report (PDF)** — pick a date range and get a polished, shareable report of your cycle and symptoms to bring to an appointment.

### 🌳 Habits that feel good to keep
- **Wellness Forest** — your consistency literally grows a tree: log your care each day and watch it bloom; skip a few days and it visibly wilts (gently, not punishingly).
- **Habits** — simple suggested routines (sleep 8 hours, eat iron-rich food, stretch) that earn reward points.
- **Streaks & rewards wallet** — every log earns points and builds a visible streak, so tracking your health feels like progress, not homework.

### 🩺 Real care, not just self-tracking
- **Doctor consultations** — book and video-call real clinical specialists directly in the app, see their live availability, chat, and get a prescription.
- **Period Buddy** — get matched with a peer for chat and video support — someone to talk to who actually gets it.
- **Community** — an anonymous-friendly forum by topic (PCOS, Fitness, Skin, and more) to ask, share, and support each other.
- **Blogs & Reels** — bite-sized educational articles and short videos on periods, hygiene, and wellness.

### 💞 Built for partners too
- **Partner Connect** — invite a partner in with a simple code to their own dedicated view.
- **You control exactly what they see** — cycle phase, intimacy notes, alerts, and reports are each a separate on/off switch. Nothing is shared by default.
- **Smart care suggestions** — instead of just showing raw data, the app translates it into real guidance for a partner ("she's feeling anxious today — a calm evening and no big decisions would help"), so caring about you doesn't require guesswork.
- **Their own space** — the partner gets a mirrored home screen, chat, and even their own access to the AI assistant and community.

### 📚 Real education, not vague articles
- **Hygiene guide** — practical advice on products, daily care, and staying comfortable during activities like swimming.
- **Sexual Wellbeing** — intimacy during periods, STI prevention, a contraception comparison, and consent & safety, all in one place.
- **Health Issues hub** — dedicated guidance for PCOS/PCOD, endometriosis, fibroids, pregnancy wellness, irregular cycles, and menopause.

### 🏛️ A genuinely unique feature: government benefits
- **Government Schemes & Benefits** — browse real Indian government health schemes (national + Rajasthan state) related to women's reproductive and menstrual health, check if you may be eligible, find your nearest government hospital, and track your own applications. This is a category of help almost no period app offers — turning awareness into access to real, often free, government support.

### 🔒 Private by design
- **App Lock** — PIN or biometric lock so your health data stays yours, even if someone picks up your phone.
- **Opt-in sharing only** — nothing about you is visible to a partner unless you explicitly turn it on.

## Why this is better than other period apps

| What people expect from a period app | What Nura adds |
|---|---|
| Logs your period, guesses the next one | Same, plus predictions personalized to *your* actual symptom history |
| A generic list of possible symptoms | A full, color-coded, easy-to-scan logging experience covering flow, mood, sex, discharge, pregnancy tests, digestion, and more |
| Static articles about periods | A conversational AI you can actually ask questions, in real time, with voice |
| Data that stays trapped in the app | An AI-written, doctor-ready PDF report you can hand to a real physician |
| No real medical help | Actual video consultations with real doctors, booked in-app |
| "Share with partner" is all-or-nothing | Granular, switch-by-switch sharing, plus a partner experience that turns your data into *actionable care advice*, not just numbers |
| Habit tracking feels like a chore | A living, growing tree that makes consistency feel rewarding |
| You're on your own | A community forum and a matched "buddy" for real peer support |
| No connection to real-world help | Direct guidance to real government health schemes and benefits you may qualify for |

**The short version:** other apps track *what's happening to you*. Nura tracks that too — but then does something about it: it explains it in plain language, connects you to real doctors, turns it into care your partner can act on, and even points you toward government support you didn't know you could get. It's built to be the app that's actually *with you*, not just watching you.
